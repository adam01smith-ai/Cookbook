Carrot Cake

Moist spiced carrot cake with cream cheese icing and optional nuts.

Method

  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
  2. 2
    Oil and line two 20cm cake tins.
  3. 3
    Whisk oil, yoghurt, eggs, vanilla and orange zest.
  4. 4
    Mix flour, sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg and a pinch of salt.
  5. 5
    Combine wet and dry ingredients.
  6. 6
    Stir in carrots, raisins and half the nuts.
  7. 7
    Divide mixture between tins.
  8. 8
    Bake for 25–30 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
  9. 9
    Cool in tins.
  10. 10
    Beat butter and sugar until smooth.
  11. 11
    Add soft cheese gradually and mix until creamy.
  12. 12
    Sandwich cakes with half the icing.
  13. 13
    Spread remaining icing on top and finish with nuts.

Add the soft cheese gradually to stop the icing splitting.

Use freshly grated carrots for moisture

Don’t overbake — keeps cake soft

Best eaten at room temperature

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Keep covered in the fridge at 0–5°C and eat within 48 hours of making. Refrigerate promptly after cooling and return leftovers to the fridge straight after serving; follow any shorter dairy use-by date.

Freezing

Freeze cooled uniced cake layers, well wrapped, for up to 2 months for best quality. Defrost in the fridge and add freshly made icing; use within 24 hours of defrosting. The soft-cheese icing is best made fresh.

Reheating

Serve chilled. Do not reheat the finished dessert. Take out only the slice you want shortly before eating so it loses its fridge chill; keep the rest refrigerated.

Substitutions

Leave out optional walnuts if preferred and check all labels if avoiding nuts. Sultanas can replace raisins. Keep full-fat soft cheese for a firmer icing; changing tin size changes baking time.

Keeping leftovers safe

Keep chilled food at 0–5°C and follow the shortest ingredient use-by or opened-pack instruction. For cooked desserts, cool promptly and refrigerate within 2 hours; do not leave them out overnight. Freeze suitable portions promptly, keep the freezer around −18°C, defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Do not refreeze a thawed serving.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
