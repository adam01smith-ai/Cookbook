Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Refrigerate covered and eat within 48 hours of cooking any pasta or chicken. Keep the dressing and watery vegetables separate where possible; add leafy greens just before eating.

Freezing

The assembled salad is best not frozen: cucumber, tomatoes and leaves lose their texture. Freeze suitable cooked meat or plain pasta separately if preparing ahead.

Reheating

Serve cold after safe chilling. If you want warm cooked components, reheat those once until steaming hot throughout before adding fresh salad and dressing.

Substitutions

Use green or red pesto to taste, and mozzarella or feta. For a vegetarian version choose pesto and cheese labelled vegetarian. Check the pesto ingredients if avoiding nuts.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
