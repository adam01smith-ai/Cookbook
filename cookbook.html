Adam Cooks on your phone

  1. iPhone: open Adam Cooks in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Choose Open as Web App if offered.
  2. Android: use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home screen option.
  3. Open a recipe and press Save for offline before you lose your connection.

The icon gives quick access; it doesn’t download recipes by itself. Your browser or device can clear stored data. Check your offline list before relying on it, and keep a collections backup.
