Truffle Rigatoni with Tenderstem Broccoli

Creamy truffle rigatoni with tenderstem broccoli, mushrooms and parmesan.

Method

  1. 1
    Cook the rigatoni in salted boiling water until al dente, about 10 minutes.
  2. 2
    Cut the tenderstem broccoli into thirds.
  3. 3
    Add the broccoli to the pasta pan 4 minutes before the pasta is cooked.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, fry the mushrooms in a little oil and season well.
  5. 5
    Cook for 4–5 minutes until golden.
  6. 6
    Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  7. 7
    Pour in the vegetable stock and crème fraîche.
  8. 8
    Allow the sauce to reduce slightly.
  9. 9
    Stir in the truffle powder and parmesan.
  10. 10
    Drain the pasta and broccoli.
  11. 11
    Add the pasta and broccoli to the sauce and toss well.
  12. 12
    Serve immediately with extra parmesan if desired.

Reserve a little pasta water to loosen the sauce if needed.

Add truffle powder gradually and taste as you go

Don't overcook the broccoli — it should still have bite

Freshly grated parmesan gives the smoothest sauce

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool promptly, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours. Label the container with the recipe name and the date and time it was made; follow any shorter ingredient use-by or opened-pack instructions.

Freezing

Freezing not recommended for the finished crème fraîche pasta. Make fresh for the best sauce and pasta texture.

Reheating

Reheat once in a pan with a splash of water, stirring until steaming hot throughout. Add fresh herbs or cheese after heating. Keep the heat gentle so the creamy sauce does not catch.

Substitutions

Use crème fraîche rather than cream for the original tangy sauce. Chestnut mushrooms work well; swap tenderstem for small broccoli florets and cook until tender.

Keeping leftovers safe

Keep chilled food at 0–5°C and follow the shortest ingredient use-by or opened-pack instruction. Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Reheat suitable cooked leftovers only once, until steaming hot throughout; ingredients already reheated in this recipe must not be reheated again. Serve chilled desserts cold. Freezing recommendations and any exceptions for a base prepared without cream or prawns are explained in this recipe’s Freezing note.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
