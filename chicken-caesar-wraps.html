Chicken Caesar Wraps

Chicken breast, crisp lettuce, Parmesan and croutons with a creamy Caesar-style dressing. Serves 2.

Time to allow: 27 min total

Prep15 mins
Cook12 mins

Method

  1. 1

    Prepare the chicken

    Slice each chicken breast horizontally into thinner cutlets if thick. Rub with the olive oil and season lightly with salt and pepper.
  2. 2

    Cook the chicken

    Heat a frying pan over a medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for about 4–6 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until golden and cooked through. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
  3. 3

    Make the dressing

    Mix all the dressing ingredients in a bowl. Taste and adjust with black pepper or a little more lemon juice if needed.
  4. 4

    Dress the lettuce

    Toss the shredded romaine with most of the dressing. Warm the tortillas briefly in a dry pan or microwave so they fold easily.
  5. 5

    Fill the wraps

    Divide the lettuce, sliced chicken, crushed croutons and remaining Parmesan between the tortillas. Spoon over the remaining dressing.
  6. 6

    Fold and serve

    Fold in the sides and roll tightly. Cut in half and serve immediately so the lettuce and croutons stay crisp.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Store the cooked chicken, dressing and washed, dried lettuce in separate covered containers in the fridge; keep croutons dry in an airtight container. Use the chilled ingredients within 48 hours of cooking the chicken. Assemble just before eating.

Freezing

Freeze cooled cooked chicken in labelled portions for up to 2 months for best quality. Tortillas can be frozen separately if the packet allows. Make the dressing and lettuce fresh and add croutons at the end; finished wraps become soggy.

Reheating

Serve the chicken cold after safe chilling, or reheat it once in a covered pan with a splash of water until steaming hot throughout. Heat the tortilla separately and add the lettuce, dressing and croutons afterwards.

Substitutions

Little gem lettuce works instead of romaine. Replace croutons with small pieces of toasted bread. Use extra mayonnaise in place of yoghurt for a richer dressing. If avoiding fish, choose an anchovy-free alternative to Worcestershire sauce and check its label.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
