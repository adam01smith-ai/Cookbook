Crispy Halloumi Pittas

Golden halloumi in warm pitta pockets with crunchy cabbage and carrot slaw and sweet chilli yoghurt. Serves 2.

Time to allow: 25 min total

Prep15 mins
Cook10 mins
2people
148

Ingredients

Pittas

Slaw

Sweet chilli yoghurt

Method

  1. 1

    Make the slaw

    Toss the shredded cabbage and grated carrot and parsley with the slaw lemon juice and black pepper. Set aside while you cook the halloumi.
  2. 2

    Mix the sauce

    Stir together the Greek yoghurt, sweet chilli sauce and remaining lemon juice.
  3. 3

    Coat the halloumi

    Pat the halloumi slices thoroughly dry with kitchen paper. Dust lightly with the cornflour, shaking off any excess.
  4. 4

    Fry until golden

    Heat the olive oil in a non-stick frying pan over a medium-high heat. Fry the halloumi for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Work in batches if needed.
  5. 5

    Warm the pittas

    Warm the pitta breads in a toaster or dry frying pan until flexible. Carefully cut open one edge of each to make a pocket.
  6. 6

    Fill and serve

    Spoon a little sweet chilli yoghurt into each pitta, then add the slaw and hot halloumi. Finish with the remaining sauce and serve immediately while the halloumi is crisp.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

For the best texture, cook the halloumi just before eating. Keep any cooked halloumi, slaw and yoghurt sauce in separate covered containers in the fridge and use within 48 hours. Fill pittas at the last moment.

Freezing

The assembled pittas are best made fresh: the vegetables soften and the yoghurt sauce may separate after freezing. Freeze spare pitta breads separately, following the packet instructions.

Reheating

Reheat cooked halloumi once in a dry non-stick pan over a medium heat, turning until steaming hot throughout. It may be firmer than when freshly fried. Warm the pitta separately, then add the cold slaw and sauce.

Substitutions

Use wraps or flatbreads instead of pittas. White cabbage works in place of red cabbage. Swap the sweet chilli sauce for a little honey and chilli flakes to taste; choose vegetarian-labelled cheese if needed.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.
