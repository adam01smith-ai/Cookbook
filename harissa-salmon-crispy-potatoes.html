Harissa Salmon with Crispy Potatoes & Lemon Yoghurt

Air-fried harissa salmon with golden oven-roasted potatoes and cool lemon yoghurt.

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 220°C / 200°C fan. Cut the potatoes into roughly 2 cm pieces.

  2. 2

    Toss the potatoes with the olive oil, cumin, salt and pepper. Spread across a baking tray and roast for 30–35 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp.

  3. 3

    Mix the harissa, honey and finely grated zest of half the lemon. Spread over the salmon.

  4. 4

    When the potatoes have about 12 minutes left, place the salmon skin-side down in the air-fryer basket, leaving space between the fillets. Start from cold and cook at 190°C for around 8–12 minutes, checking from 8 minutes, until cooked through and flaking easily. No flipping is needed. The honey can darken quickly, so check during cooking.

  5. 5

    Mix the yoghurt with the garlic, a squeeze of lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

  6. 6

    Spoon the lemon yoghurt onto plates. Add the crispy potatoes and salmon, then finish with parsley, if using, and lemon wedges.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool in shallow portions and refrigerate covered within 2 hours. Eat within 48 hours. Keep yoghurt sauce separate and eat leftovers within 24 hours for best quality.

Freezing

Freeze cooled individual portions, tightly wrapped, for up to 2 months for best quality. Defrost fully in the fridge. Freeze cooled fish and potatoes separately from the yoghurt sauce; fish becomes firmer and potatoes less crisp.

Reheating

Reheat once in an oven at 180°C / 160°C fan until steaming hot in the centre. Cover if the top browns before the middle is hot; portion size affects the time. Check the salmon is hot in the centre, not just warm on the surface; add cold yoghurt sauce afterwards.

Substitutions

Use the salmon fillets as written; adjust cooking for their thickness. Reduce harissa for less heat. Plain yoghurt works instead of Greek yoghurt but makes a looser sauce.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
