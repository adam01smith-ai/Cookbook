Harissa Salmon with Crispy Potatoes & Lemon Yoghurt

Air-fried harissa salmon with golden oven-roasted potatoes and cool lemon yoghurt.

Prep10 mins
Cook35 mins

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 220°C / 200°C fan. Cut the potatoes into roughly 2 cm pieces.

  2. 2

    Toss the potatoes with the olive oil, cumin, salt and pepper. Spread across a baking tray and roast for 30–35 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp.

  3. 3

    Mix the harissa, honey and finely grated zest of half the lemon. Spread over the salmon.

  4. 4

    When the potatoes have about 12 minutes left, place the salmon skin-side down in the air-fryer basket, leaving space between the fillets. Start from cold and cook at 190°C for around 8–12 minutes, checking from 8 minutes, until cooked through and flaking easily. No flipping is needed. The honey can darken quickly, so check during cooking.

  5. 5

    Mix the yoghurt with the garlic, a squeeze of lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

  6. 6

    Spoon the lemon yoghurt onto plates. Add the crispy potatoes and salmon, then finish with parsley, if using, and lemon wedges.
