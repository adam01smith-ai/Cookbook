Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Store cooled cookies in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. For best texture, eat within 3 days. Do not seal while warm or the trapped steam will soften them.

Freezing

Freeze cooled cookies, separated with parchment, for up to 2 months for best quality. Thaw only the number wanted in their covered container. Raw dough portions can also be frozen if tightly wrapped; bake through before eating.

Reheating

Eat at room temperature, or warm baked cookies briefly in a low oven until the chocolate softens. They can dry out quickly; no need to make them piping hot like a meat dish.

Substitutions

Use chopped chocolate instead of chips. Keep the flour, butter and sugar balance for the same spread; a different sugar mix changes chewiness. Do not eat raw dough containing flour or egg.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool baked cookies fully before sealing to avoid condensation. Use clean containers and keep them dry. Do not eat raw flour or raw cookie dough. If adding fresh cream or another perishable topping, refrigerate and follow that ingredient’s shorter storage instructions.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
