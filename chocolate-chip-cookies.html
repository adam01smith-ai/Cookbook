Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft in the middle, slightly crisp on the edges, loaded with chocolate chunks.

Method

  1. 1
    Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  2. 2
    Beat in egg and vanilla.
  3. 3
    Fold in flour, baking powder, chocolate and salt.
  4. 4
    Mix gently — do not overwork the dough.
  5. 5
    Chill dough overnight for best flavour (optional but recommended).
  6. 6
    Preheat oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
  7. 7
    Roll into rough balls and space on baking trays.
  8. 8
    Bake:
    • Fresh dough: 8–10 minutes
    • Chilled dough: 10–12 minutes
  9. 9
    Bake until golden at edges but soft in the centre.
  10. 10
    Cool on tray briefly before transferring.

Cookies will firm up as they cool — don’t overbake.

Chilling improves flavour and texture

Rough dough = better looking cookies

Slightly underbake for soft centres
