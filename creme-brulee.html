Crème Brûlée

Classic creamy custard with a crisp caramelised sugar topping.

4person
2345678

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
  2. 2
    Place ramekins in a deep roasting tin.
  3. 3
    Heat cream and milk in a pan.
  4. 4
    Split vanilla pod and scrape seeds into the cream.
  5. 5
    Whisk egg yolks and sugar until pale.
  6. 6
    Heat cream until just below boiling.
  7. 7
    Slowly pour hot cream into eggs, whisking.
  8. 8
    Strain mixture through a sieve.
  9. 9
    Remove foam from the surface.
  10. 10
    Pour mixture into ramekins.
  11. 11
    Add hot water to the tray (bain-marie).
  12. 12
    Bake 30–35 minutes until just set with a slight wobble.
  13. 13
    Cool slightly, then chill in fridge until fully set.
  14. 14
    Sprinkle sugar evenly over the top.
  15. 15
    Use a blowtorch to caramelise until crisp.

The centre should wobble slightly — overbaking makes it firm.

Use a bain-marie for even cooking

Chill overnight for best texture

Caramelise just before serving
