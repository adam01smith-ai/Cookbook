Slow Cooker Chicken Chasseur

chicken breast, mushrooms and carrots in a rich red wine sauce, finished with butter. Original recipe serves 4; quantities default to 6 servings.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Soften the shallots and garlic

    Select Sauté on your Instant Pot or slow cooker, using a medium heat. If your slow cooker has no sauté setting, use a frying pan for this step, then transfer everything to the cooker. Add 1.5 tsp olive oil and fry 4.5 sliced banana shallots (or 1.5 onion, sliced) for 5 minutes. Add 4.5 chopped garlic cloves and fry for 2 minutes, then turn off Sauté.
  2. 2

    Add the chicken and vegetables

    Add 750 g skinless, boneless chicken breast, 300 g button mushrooms, 3 large carrots cut into batons, 3 thyme sprigs, 1.5 bay leaf and 3 tbsp tomato purée to the cooker.
  3. 3

    Mix the sauce and slow cook

    Mix 1.5 low-salt chicken stock cube with 225 ml water and 1.5 tsp cornflour. Stir in 337.5 ml red wine, then pour into the cooker and cover. Select Slow Cook, Low (called Less on some Instant Pot models), and cook for about 3–4 hours. Start checking from 3 hours: the thickest part must reach 75°C for at least 30 seconds. Continue cooking if needed.
  4. 4

    Check the sauce

    Check the liquid level towards the end of cooking. If there is too much sauce, reduce it once the chicken is cooked, using the finish below. Use the lid as directed by your appliance manufacturer.
  5. 5

    Finish with butter

    Once cooking has finished, if the sauce is too watery, use Sauté for a few minutes to reduce it, or transfer the sauce to a saucepan and simmer. Remove the thyme stalks and bay leaf. Stir in 37.5 g butter, taste and season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately. Cool leftovers promptly and keep in the fridge for up to 48 hours.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Divide into shallow containers to cool, refrigerate within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions with their sauce for up to 2 months for best quality. Label before freezing. Freeze before the butter finish if preparing ahead; stir in butter after reheating.

Reheating

Reheat once on the hob or in the microwave until steaming hot throughout; stir and turn meat pieces so the centre heats evenly. Do not use the slow cooker to reheat chilled leftovers. Reheat gently in the sauce so the breast stays as moist as possible.

Substitutions

Keep chicken breast as the meat. Use a sliced onion instead of banana shallots. Chicken stock can replace red wine; add a little lemon juice at the end to taste.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
