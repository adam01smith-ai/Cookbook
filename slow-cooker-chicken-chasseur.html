Slow Cooker Chicken Chasseur

Chicken thighs, mushrooms and carrots in a rich red wine sauce, finished with butter. Original recipe serves 4; quantities default to 6 servings.

Prep15 mins
Slow cookLow 6 hrs
6people

Method

  1. 1

    Soften the shallots and garlic

    Select Sauté on your Instant Pot or slow cooker, using a medium heat. If your slow cooker has no sauté setting, use a frying pan for this step, then transfer everything to the cooker. Add 1.5 tsp olive oil and fry 4.5 sliced banana shallots (or 1.5 onion, sliced) for 5 minutes. Add 4.5 chopped garlic cloves and fry for 2 minutes, then turn off Sauté.
  2. 2

    Add the chicken and vegetables

    Add 750 g skinless, boneless chicken thighs, 300 g button mushrooms, 3 large carrots cut into batons, 3 thyme sprigs, 1.5 bay leaf and 3 tbsp tomato purée to the cooker.
  3. 3

    Mix the sauce and slow cook

    Mix 1.5 low-salt chicken stock cube with 225 ml water and 1.5 tsp cornflour. Stir in 337.5 ml red wine, then pour into the cooker and cover. Select Slow Cook, Low (called Less on some Instant Pot models), and cook for about 6 hours, until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
  4. 4

    Check the sauce

    Check the liquid level towards the end of cooking. If there is too much sauce, reduce it once the chicken is cooked, using the finish below. Use the lid as directed by your appliance manufacturer.
  5. 5

    Finish with butter

    Once cooking has finished, if the sauce is too watery, use Sauté for a few minutes to reduce it, or transfer the sauce to a saucepan and simmer. Remove the thyme stalks and bay leaf. Stir in 37.5 g butter, taste and season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately. Cool leftovers promptly and keep in the fridge for up to 48 hours.
