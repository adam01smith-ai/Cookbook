Beef köfte, homemade flatbreads & garlic yoghurt

Fragrant beef köfte with soft pan-cooked flatbreads, cool garlic yoghurt and a lemony tomato-and-cucumber salad. No lamb.

Method

  1. 1

    Make the flatbread dough

    Mix the plain flour, Greek yoghurt, baking powder and salt from the Four small flatbreads section into a soft dough. Knead briefly, cover and rest for 15 minutes. Keep the separate measured dusting flour aside for rolling.
  2. 2

    Shape the köfte

    Mix the beef mince with the squeezed grated onion, garlic, fresh parsley, ground cumin, ground coriander, smoked paprika, salt and black pepper from the Beef köfte section. Mix until the meat holds together, then shape into 6 short, evenly sized oval köfte. Chill while preparing the sides.
  3. 3

    Make yoghurt and salad

    Mix all the Garlic yoghurt ingredients in one bowl. In another bowl, toss the cucumber, tomatoes and red onion with the lemon juice, olive oil, most of the fresh parsley and salt from the Salad section. Save a pinch of that parsley to finish the flatbreads. Keep the yoghurt chilled until serving.
  4. 4

    Cook the flatbreads

    Divide the dough into 4 equal balls, allowing two small flatbreads per person. Dust the worktop with the reserved plain flour and roll each ball to about 3mm thick. Heat a dry non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat and cook each flatbread for 2–3 minutes per side until browned in patches and cooked through. Finish with the reserved pinch of fresh parsley and wrap in a clean tea towel to keep warm.
  5. 5

    Cook the köfte

    Heat the olive oil from the Beef köfte section in a separate frying pan over medium heat. Cook the köfte for 9–12 minutes, turning regularly, until browned all around and the centres reach 75°C for 30 seconds. Cook in batches if needed.
  6. 6

    Serve

    Serve three köfte per person at the default two servings, with two small flatbreads each, the garlic yoghurt and salad. Divide the scaled batch evenly when changing servings.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool cooked köfte and refrigerate within 2 hours; use within 48 hours. Keep yoghurt and salad chilled separately, following the yoghurt’s opened-pack instructions if shorter. Flatbreads are best fresh; store cooled bread airtight.

Freezing

Freeze cooled cooked köfte and flatbreads separately. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Make yoghurt and salad fresh.

Reheating

Reheat köfte only once until steaming hot through the middle. Warm flatbreads briefly in a dry pan. Keep the yoghurt and salad cold.

Substitutions

Use all-beef mince as written; no lamb is needed. For an easier version, use two large shop-bought flatbreads for two people and omit the homemade-flatbread ingredients.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
