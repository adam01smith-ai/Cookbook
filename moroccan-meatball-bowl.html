Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool rice as quickly as possible, ideally within 1 hour, and refrigerate in shallow portions. Eat the rice, and any leftovers mixed with it, within 24 hours. Keep other cooked components separate and use within 48 hours. Keep yoghurt and chopped salad separate from the meatballs and rice.

Freezing

Freeze suitable cooled components separately for up to 2 months for best quality. If freezing freshly cooked rice, cool it quickly and freeze within 1 hour. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Freeze meatballs in their tomato sauce; add yoghurt and chopped salad fresh.

Reheating

Reheat rice only once until steaming hot throughout and eat immediately. Heat other cooked components thoroughly, keeping cold toppings separate. Check the middle of the meatballs is steaming hot; add yoghurt and fresh vegetables after heating.

Substitutions

Use beef mince or lamb mince in the meatballs. Panko works instead of fresh breadcrumbs if needed; moisten it first. Cook any alternative rice using its own packet ratio and timing.

Keeping leftovers safe

Rice needs faster cooling: cool quickly, ideally within 1 hour, and refrigerate; use within 24 hours. If freezing freshly cooked rice, cool it quickly and freeze within 1 hour. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once until steaming hot throughout and eat immediately. Do not heat rice again if it was already reheated, including a heated microwave pouch. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers · cooked rice.
