Method

  1. 1

    Add the frozen acai, frozen banana and frozen berries to a blender.

  2. 2

    Add the milk and blend slowly.

  3. 3

    Only add a little more liquid if needed. Keep the mixture thick.

  4. 4

    Continue blending until smooth and scoopable, like soft ice cream.

  5. 5

    Spoon the thick acai mixture into a bowl.

  6. 6

    Arrange the granola, sliced banana, fresh berries, chia seeds and coconut flakes on top. Add the optional honey if using and serve immediately.
