Acai Bowl

A thick smoothie-style bowl made with açaí powder, ripe banana and frozen berries, finished with crunchy toppings.

Method

  1. 1

    Add the açaí powder, ripe banana and frozen mixed berries to a high-powered blender or food processor.

  2. 2

    Add half the measured milk and pulse to start breaking up the frozen fruit. Stop and scrape down the sides as needed.

  3. 3

    Add the remaining milk a little at a time, only as needed to help the blades turn. You may not need it all. Keep the mixture thick.

  4. 4

    Continue blending until smooth and thick enough to spoon. Use the frozen berries straight from the freezer to keep the bowl cold and thick.

  5. 5

    Divide the thick açaí mixture between bowls.

  6. 6

    Arrange the granola, sliced banana, fresh berries, chia seeds and coconut flakes on top. Add the optional honey if using and serve immediately.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Best blended and eaten straight away. Refrigerate any untopped leftovers promptly in a covered container and use within 24 hours; the colour and texture will change. Keep granola and toppings separate.

Freezing

Freeze the untopped blended base promptly in small sealed portions for up to 1 month for best texture. Thaw in the fridge just enough to blend again, and use within 24 hours of thawing. Check the frozen-fruit packet for any handling instructions.

Reheating

Do not heat the finished bowl. If frozen berries require cooking on their packet, cook them first and chill safely before blending. Add fresh banana and crunchy toppings when serving.

Substitutions

Keep açaí powder and a normal ripe banana as written. Use your usual milk or unsweetened plant drink, and adjust gradually. Swap berries to taste; choose toppings that suit any allergies.

Keeping leftovers safe

Keep chilled food at 0–5°C and follow the shortest ingredient use-by or opened-pack instruction. For cooked desserts, cool promptly and refrigerate within 2 hours; do not leave them out overnight. Freeze suitable portions promptly, keep the freezer around −18°C, defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Do not refreeze a thawed serving.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
