Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Chop the onions, carrot, tomatoes, pepper, fresh coriander, garlic and ginger, and put in a pan.

  2. 2

    Cover with water and add salt and black pepper. Bring to the boil and simmer until the ingredients are soft.

  3. 3

    Remove from the heat and add the tin of chopped tomatoes. Blend until smooth.

  4. 4

    Add all the spices and return to the heat. Simmer for a further 20 minutes.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool in shallow containers, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions in sealed, labelled containers for up to 2 months for best quality. Leave expansion space. Freeze in recipe-sized portions so you can thaw only what you need.

Reheating

Reheat once in a saucepan, stirring and adding a splash of water or stock, until steaming hot throughout. Only heat the portion needed for the curry; do not repeatedly warm the whole batch.

Substitutions

Use brown onions if Spanish onions are unavailable. Keep the stock-free vegetable base mild so individual curries can be seasoned separately. Blend thoroughly for a smooth gravy.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
