🥜 Satay Beef Noodles
🍜 Noodles 🥜 Satay 🌶️ Spicy

Servings: 2

Sweet, spicy satay-style beef noodles with crunchy peanuts and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • red pepper
  • lime
  • garlic clove
  • nests noodles
  • tbsp peanut butter
  • tbsp ketjap manis
  • tbsp chilli sauce
  • tbsp soy sauce
  • red onion
  • g beef mince
  • handful mangetout
  • tbsp peanuts

Method

  1. Thinly slice the red pepper and red onion.
  2. Zest the lime and cut the remaining lime into wedges.
  3. Crush or finely chop the peanuts.
  4. Brown the beef mince in a large pan, then remove and set aside.
  5. Cook the noodles according to packet instructions.
  6. Add onion and pepper to the pan and cook until softened.
  7. Add the mangetout and cook for 1–2 minutes.
  8. In a bowl, mix together the peanut butter, ketjap manis, chilli sauce, soy sauce and lime zest.
  9. Return the beef mince to the pan.
  10. Add the cooked noodles.
  11. Pour over the sauce and toss everything together until coated.
  12. Serve with crushed peanuts and lime wedges.

Add a splash of water if the sauce becomes too thick.

Tips

  • Use crunchy peanut butter for extra texture
  • Add extra chilli sauce if you like more heat
  • Fresh lime juice brightens the finished dish
