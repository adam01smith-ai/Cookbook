Slow Cooker Tuscan Chicken

Tender chicken breast in a creamy garlic and Parmesan sauce with sun-dried tomatoes and spinach.

Method

  1. 1

    Put the onion, garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, stock, tomato purée, oregano, paprika and a little black pepper into the slow cooker. Stir together, then add the chicken breast and turn to coat. No frying is needed.

  2. 2

    Cover and cook on low for about 3–4 hours, until the chicken is tender and cooked through. Timings vary with the cooker and size of the fillets; a clean thermometer inserted into the thickest part should read 75°C for at least 30 seconds.

  3. 3

    Mix the cornflour with the cold water until smooth, then whisk in the double cream. Gently stir this mixture into the sauce in the slow cooker, lifting the chicken aside briefly if needed.

  4. 4

    Stir in the Parmesan and fold in the spinach. Cover and switch to high for about 20–30 minutes, until the sauce is hot and thickened, the cheese has melted and the spinach has wilted. Stir gently once during this time; avoid prolonged vigorous boiling.

  5. 5

    Taste before adding salt, as the stock, tomatoes and Parmesan are already salty. Add the optional lemon juice a little at a time if you would like a fresher finish. Scatter over basil if using and serve straight from the slow cooker.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Divide into shallow containers to cool, refrigerate within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours. Label the container with the recipe name and the date and time it was made; follow any shorter ingredient use-by or opened-pack instructions.

Freezing

Freezing not recommended for the finished dish with cream, Parmesan and spinach. Make fresh for the best sauce texture.

Reheating

Reheat once on the hob or in the microwave until steaming hot throughout; stir and turn meat pieces so the centre heats evenly. Do not use the slow cooker to reheat chilled leftovers. Use gentle heat and stir the sauce; avoid a rolling boil that can split the cream.

Substitutions

Keep chicken breast as the meat. Use kale instead of spinach, adding it earlier so it softens. Crème fraîche can replace cream, giving a tangier finish.

Keeping leftovers safe

Keep chilled food at 0–5°C and follow the shortest ingredient use-by or opened-pack instruction. Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Reheat suitable cooked leftovers only once, until steaming hot throughout; ingredients already reheated in this recipe must not be reheated again. Serve chilled desserts cold. Freezing recommendations and any exceptions for a base prepared without cream or prawns are explained in this recipe’s Freezing note.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
