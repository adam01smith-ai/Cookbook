Coconutty Caribbean Soup

Warming Caribbean-inspired soup with roasted sweet potato, coconut milk and jerk spice.

Estimated total: About 30 min totalAir-fryer method: about 20 minutes for the sweet potato while the soup cooks in parallel. Original full ingredient batch serves 4; quantities shown default to 2. Oven roasting or air-fryer batches take longer.

Method

  1. 1
    Heat the air fryer to 200°C if your model needs preheating. Peel the sweet potatoes and cut into even 2 cm cubes. The air-fryer route takes about 30 minutes overall when you prepare the soup in parallel.
  2. 2
    Toss the sweet potato with half the measured olive oil and a little salt and black pepper. Spread in the basket without packing it tightly; use both drawers if available. Larger quantities may need batches and take longer.
  3. 3
    Air-fry for about 20 minutes, shaking halfway through, until tender all the way through and lightly golden. Test a piece with a knife; allow a few extra minutes if needed. Start the saucepan while it cooks.
  4. 4
    While the potato cooks, slice the spring onions, keeping a little of the green part for the garnish. Dice the red pepper and finely chop the garlic. Drain and rinse the black beans.
  5. 5
    Heat the remaining measured olive oil in a large saucepan over a medium heat for about 1 minute.
  6. 6
    Add the spring onions, except the reserved garnish, and the red pepper. Cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring, until softened.
  7. 7
    Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, stirring so it does not burn.
  8. 8
    Stir in the measured jerk seasoning and curry powder. Cook for 30 seconds, stirring, until fragrant. Jerk blends vary in salt and heat; use less initially if yours is particularly hot.
  9. 9
    Add the drained black beans and chopped tomatoes, and stir to combine.
  10. 10
    Pour in the measured vegetable stock and coconut milk. Bring just to a gentle simmer over a medium heat, which should take about 3–5 minutes.
  11. 11
    Lower the heat and simmer gently, uncovered, for 8–10 minutes while the sweet potato finishes cooking. Stir occasionally; avoid boiling hard.
  12. 12
    Add the cooked sweet potato and stir gently. Simmer for a further 2–3 minutes until the potato and soup are hot throughout.
  13. 13
    For a thicker texture, mash a few sweet potato pieces against the side of the saucepan, or blend part of the soup and stir it back in. Leave the rest chunky.
  14. 14
    Finely zest the lime before juicing. Stir in juice from the lime quantity shown in the ingredient list, adding it gradually to taste. Check the seasoning and add salt and black pepper as needed.
  15. 15
    Serve topped with the reserved lime zest and spring onion. Oven alternative: roast the sweet potato at 200°C conventional / 180°C fan for about 30 minutes until tender, preparing the saucepan in parallel. Allow extra time compared with the air-fryer route.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool in shallow containers, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions in sealed, labelled containers for up to 2 months for best quality. Leave expansion space. Stir to recombine the coconut milk after thawing.

Reheating

Reheat once in a saucepan, stirring and adding a splash of water or stock, until steaming hot throughout. Keep the sweet potato pieces intact by stirring gently.

Substitutions

Use squash instead of sweet potato, roasting until tender. Kidney beans can replace black beans. Jerk seasoning varies in salt and heat; start with less and adjust.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.
