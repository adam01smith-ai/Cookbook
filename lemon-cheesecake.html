Lemon Cheesecake

Creamy lemon cheesecake with a biscuit base and fresh raspberry topping.

2person
4914

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
  2. 2
    Line a 23cm springform tin with greaseproof paper.
  3. 3
    Blitz biscuits and mix with melted butter.
  4. 4
    Press into the base and chill.
  5. 5
    Whisk mascarpone, soft cheese, eggs, yolks, lemon zest and juice.
  6. 6
    Add flour and sugar and mix until smooth.
  7. 7
    Pour into the tin.
  8. 8
    Bake for 35–40 minutes until slightly wobbly in the centre.
  9. 9
    Turn off the oven and leave the door ajar briefly to let the initial heat escape. Remove the cheesecake, cool promptly and refrigerate within 2 hours of finishing baking; do not leave it in the oven for several hours.
  10. 10
    Remove from tin and spread over soured cream.
  11. 11
    Swirl lemon curd on top.
  12. 12
    Decorate with raspberries if using.

Brief gentle cooling helps reduce cracking; refrigerate promptly for food safety.

Tips & leftovers

Storage

Keep covered in the fridge at 0–5°C and eat within 48 hours of making. Refrigerate promptly after cooling and return leftovers to the fridge straight after serving; follow any shorter dairy use-by date.

Freezing

Freeze cooled plain slices without soured cream, lemon curd or berries, well wrapped, for up to 1 month for best texture. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours; add the toppings fresh. The filling may be slightly less smooth after thawing.

Reheating

Serve chilled. Do not reheat the finished dessert.

Substitutions

Use digestive or plain oat biscuits for the base. Keep full-fat soft cheese and mascarpone for the intended set. Lime can replace lemon, added to taste rather than assuming the same number of fruit.

Keeping leftovers safe

Keep chilled food at 0–5°C and follow the shortest ingredient use-by or opened-pack instruction. For cooked desserts, cool promptly and refrigerate within 2 hours; do not leave them out overnight. Freeze suitable portions promptly, keep the freezer around −18°C, defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Do not refreeze a thawed serving.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
