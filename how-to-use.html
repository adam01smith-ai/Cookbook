How to use Adam Cooks

Find dinner, plan your week and keep the recipes you love. No account needed.

Find your next meal

On the recipe index, search by name or ingredients. Enter several ingredients to find recipes containing all of them. Choose a category and use the time filter to narrow your choices; timings are estimates and include extra waiting where shown.

Open a recipe and adjust its servings. Ingredient quantities update automatically. Tick ingredients as you prepare them.

Plan your week and build a shopping list

  1. Open the weekly planner. Choose your start date, number of people and how many dinners you want suggested.
  2. Suggest dinners. Swap dishes, choose a recipe yourself or press Keep to preserve a meal when suggesting again.
  3. Want extra for lunch or another time? Increase Extra portions on that meal. For two people, two extra portions means cook four. Other meals keep their own quantities.
  4. To eat a dish again later that week, make the later day Leftovers and link it to the earlier cooking day. Those portions are added automatically; Extra portions are additional to linked leftovers.
  5. Choose sides and answer the base-gravy question if a curry needs it. Then press Confirm week & build shopping list.
  6. Tick ingredients you already have, add any extra shopping, and copy or print your list.

To retain plans between visits, enable Remember my planner in Privacy settings. Export a planner backup before moving devices or clearing browser data. Check each recipe’s storage and freezing instructions before keeping leftovers.

Keep recipes for next time

  • Favourites: tap the heart on a recipe or card. Open My cookbook → My favourites to find them.
  • Collections: use Add to collection on a recipe, or create a list in My cookbook. Export a collections backup to download or copy; import it on another device.
  • Recently viewed: turn on Remember recipes I view in My cookbook to keep the last eight recipe visits. You can pause it or clear the list.
  • Private notes: save your own note beneath a recipe. It stays in that browser and is not posted publicly. Collection backups do not include notes.

Cook one step at a time

Press Enter cooking mode on a recipe for larger text, one step at a time, and an ingredient reminder. Use Next step and Previous to move through the method. Timers continue when you move between steps or leave cooking mode.

Test the finishing chime and check your device volume. Sound and Keep screen awake depend on your browser and device; a locked screen or background app may delay timer alerts. Keep the cooking screen open while relying on a timer.

Save recipes offline and add a home-screen icon

  1. While connected, open a recipe and press Save for offline. Wait for the saved confirmation.
  2. An optional invitation offers to add Adam Cooks to your home screen. Choose Maybe later to hide the invitation for 14 days; installation help remains in My cookbook.
  3. iPhone or iPad: open the site in Safari, tap Safari’s Share button → Add to Home Screen. Choose Open as Web App if offered, then Add.
  4. Android or computer: use Install Adam Cooks when offered, or your browser menu’s Install app / Add to Home screen option.
  5. Open the installed app while connected and check My cookbook → Available offline. If the app has separate saved data, save the recipes there too.

The icon gives quick access; it does not download every recipe. Each saved recipe includes its photo, ingredients, serving controls, cooking mode and timers. Vibe cooking, sending feedback and unsaved recipes need a connection. Use Update copy to refresh a download when online.

Deleting an offline copy leaves favourites, collections and notes intact. Browser storage management or clearing site/app data can remove downloads, so check the offline list before relying on it.

Share a recipe with its picture

Press Share recipe to see the photo and link together. Choose Share link or Copy link to send the recipe address. On supported phones, Share photo & link attaches the photo too; check that the link is included before sending.

Your phone and the receiving app decide how a normal link preview appears. Photo sharing sends an image attachment and may appear differently from a link card. Neither option sends your private notes or saved plans.

Your saved items belong to your browser

You see your own saved meals, favourites, notes and collections. Other visitors see theirs. People sharing the same browser profile share that profile’s saved items. Browsers, devices and installed apps do not automatically sync.
