Korean Style Gochujang Beef and Pork Bibimbap

Jasmine rice topped with sticky gochujang beef and pork, carrot ribbons and sesame green beans.

2person

Method

  1. 1
    Peel and trim the carrot. Use a vegetable peeler to make long ribbons down its length. Place in a bowl, add the rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar, and toss. Leave to pickle while you cook.
  2. 2
    Trim the green beans. Heat a drizzle of oil in a pan over a medium heat, then stir-fry the beans until slightly charred, about 2–3 minutes.
  3. 3
    Add the garlic, sprinkle over the sesame seeds and add a splash of water. Cook until the beans are tender, then transfer to a bowl and keep warm.
  4. 4
    Return the pan to the heat. Add the beef and pork mince and fry, breaking it up, until browned and cooked through, about 5–6 minutes or as needed.
  5. 5
    Stir in the gochujang paste, soy sauce, sweet chilli sauce, honey and measured water.
  6. 6
    Simmer until the sauce has thickened. Meanwhile, microwave the jasmine rice according to its packet instructions, about 2 minutes.
  7. 7
    Divide the rice between bowls. Arrange the gochujang mince, pickled carrot ribbons and sesame green beans in separate sections on top.
