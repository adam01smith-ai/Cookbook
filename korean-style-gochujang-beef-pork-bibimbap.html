Korean Style Gochujang Beef and Pork Bibimbap

Jasmine rice topped with sticky gochujang beef and pork, carrot ribbons and sesame green beans.

Method

  1. 1
    Peel and trim the carrot. Use a vegetable peeler to make long ribbons down its length. Place in a bowl, add the rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar, and toss. Leave to pickle while you cook.
  2. 2
    Trim the green beans. Heat a drizzle of oil in a pan over a medium heat, then stir-fry the beans until slightly charred, about 2–3 minutes.
  3. 3
    Add the garlic, sprinkle over the sesame seeds and add a splash of water. Cook until the beans are tender, then transfer to a bowl and keep warm.
  4. 4
    Return the pan to the heat. Add the beef and pork mince and fry, breaking it up, until browned and cooked through, about 5–6 minutes or as needed.
  5. 5
    Stir in the gochujang paste, soy sauce, sweet chilli sauce, honey and measured water.
  6. 6
    Simmer until the sauce has thickened. Meanwhile, microwave the jasmine rice according to its packet instructions, about 2 minutes.
  7. 7
    Divide the rice between bowls. Arrange the gochujang mince, pickled carrot ribbons and sesame green beans in separate sections on top.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool rice as quickly as possible, ideally within 1 hour, and refrigerate in shallow portions. Eat the rice, and any leftovers mixed with it, within 24 hours. Keep other cooked components separate and use within 48 hours. For microwave rice, follow the packet; once heated, do not heat it again.

Freezing

Freeze suitable cooled components separately for up to 2 months for best quality. If freezing freshly cooked rice, cool it quickly and freeze within 1 hour. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Use freshly cooked rice for portions intended for reheating; do not refreeze or save already reheated rice for another hot meal.

Reheating

Reheat rice only once until steaming hot throughout and eat immediately. Heat other cooked components thoroughly, keeping cold toppings separate. The recipe uses microwave rice: if that rice has already been heated, make fresh rice instead of reheating it again. Reheat the mince separately.

Substitutions

Use all-beef mince instead of the beef-and-pork mix. Broccoli can replace green beans. Gochujang brands vary, so add gradually rather than replacing it spoon-for-spoon with a much hotter chilli paste.

Keeping leftovers safe

Rice needs faster cooling: cool quickly, ideally within 1 hour, and refrigerate; use within 24 hours. If freezing freshly cooked rice, cool it quickly and freeze within 1 hour. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once until steaming hot throughout and eat immediately. Do not heat rice again if it was already reheated, including a heated microwave pouch. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers · cooked rice.
