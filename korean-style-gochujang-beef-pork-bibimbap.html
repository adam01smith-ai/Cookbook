🍚 Korean Style Gochujang Beef and Pork Bibimbap

🍚 Rice & Bowls🌶️ Gochujang🥕 Pickled carrot

Jasmine rice topped with sticky gochujang beef and pork, carrot ribbons and sesame green beans.

Ingredients

  • Microwave jasmine rice
  • 15 ml rice vinegar
  • Green beans
  • 240 g beef and pork mince
  • 15 ml soy sauce
  • 32 g sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 clove garlic, peeled and minced
  • 5 g sesame seeds
  • 50 g gochujang paste
  • 10 g honey
  • 50 ml water
  • A drizzle of oil, for frying
  • A pinch of sugar, for the carrot pickle
  • A splash of water, for the beans

Serves 2 as written. Rice and green bean quantities were not specified; use enough for your chosen servings. The slider scales the measured ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel and trim the carrot. Use a vegetable peeler to make long ribbons down its length. Place in a bowl, add the rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar, and toss. Leave to pickle while you cook.
  2. Trim the green beans. Heat a drizzle of oil in a pan over a medium heat, then stir-fry the beans until slightly charred, about 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add the garlic, sprinkle over the sesame seeds and add a splash of water. Cook until the beans are tender, then transfer to a bowl and keep warm.
  4. Return the pan to the heat. Add the beef and pork mince and fry, breaking it up, until browned and cooked through, about 5–6 minutes or as needed.
  5. Stir in the gochujang paste, soy sauce, sweet chilli sauce, honey and measured water.
  6. Simmer until the sauce has thickened. Meanwhile, microwave the jasmine rice according to its packet instructions, about 2 minutes.
  7. Divide the rice between bowls. Arrange the gochujang mince, pickled carrot ribbons and sesame green beans in separate sections on top.
