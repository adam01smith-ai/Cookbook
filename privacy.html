Privacy & storage.

Updated 10 November 2026. Adam Cooks is a recipe website run by Adam Smith.

You can browse recipes, use the planner and request AI meal ideas without accepting optional browser saving or analytics. at any time.

What this device remembers

We use localStorage, a form of browser storage covered by similar rules to cookies. Google Analytics cookies are only used if you accept analytics. Advertising cookies are not currently enabled.

Optional data is only read or updated with your permission. Turning saving off preserves older saved data without reading it. Use “Clear saved plans and prompts” in privacy settings to delete it. Clearing browser site data also removes it. Without saving, your current page works in memory; leaving or reloading may lose the plan and repeat avoidance will not carry into future visits. You can use the planner's export/import backup controls instead.

These choices apply to this browser and origin, not every device. Your browser may separately cache website files and food images for loading; we do not use that cache to track you.

Saved favourites

Clicking a recipe heart asks us to save that recipe in this browser, using localStorage key adam-cooks-favourites-v1. This stores recipe filenames only, stays until you remove the recipes or clear browser site data, and does not sync between devices or send your favourites to Adam. Clicking the heart again removes a recipe. This requested saving is separate from optional planner, prompt and analytics settings; rejecting those does not erase favourites. If browser storage is unavailable, favourites work only on the current page.

Your cooking notes

Each recipe has a My cooking notes box. Pressing “Save notes on this device” requests local browser saving, using localStorage key adam-cooks-recipe-notes-v1. It stores your note text, recipe filename and the time you saved it. Notes stay in this browser until you clear them or remove browser site data; they do not sync between devices, go to Adam or become public comments. Anyone using the same browser profile on your device may be able to see them, so avoid sensitive personal information.

Use “Clear this note” on a recipe to delete that recipe’s note while keeping notes for other dishes. Unsaved edits stay on the current page and may be lost when you leave. As with saved favourites, pressing Save is a separate request from optional planner, prompt and analytics settings; rejecting those optional settings does not erase your saved recipe notes. Our analytics code does not send note text, and printing a recipe omits the notes and feedback section.

The private feedback link is separate: it opens FormSubmit in a new tab with the recipe title, link and chosen topic in its subject. Your saved cooking notes are never added to that link. You choose what message and reply details to submit; feedback is sent to Adam rather than posted publicly.

When you request AI meal ideas

Pressing “Find my vibe” sends your cooking brief, use/avoid ingredients, servings and fish preference to our Cloudflare-hosted service, then to OpenAI to generate ideas. We also provide existing cookbook recipe titles to help make the ideas different. Copying a prompt to ChatGPT instead only copies text; you choose whether to paste and send it there.

We use the information to answer your request and operate and protect the service, independently of optional device saving. Please do not enter names, contact details, medical records or other sensitive personal information. AI suggestions cannot guarantee allergen safety.

Our service uses your IP address for rate limiting and stores daily/monthly request counts in Cloudflare's database. Those counters do not contain cooking prompts. Our request to OpenAI has response storage disabled, but this does not mean providers retain no operational or security data. Providers may process data outside the UK under their applicable safeguards and terms.

Read OpenAI's API data information and Cloudflare's privacy policy for their processing and retention information.

Private contact messages

Our Contact page links to a form hosted by FormSubmit. Opening that form shares technical request information with FormSubmit and any services it loads. When you submit it, your name, reply email, subject and message are processed by FormSubmit and sent to Adam's inbox to handle your enquiry. Messages are not published on Adam Cooks. Contact details and message text are not sent by our website analytics code.

FormSubmit says it retains submissions for 30 days. Delivered messages remain in Adam's inbox until deleted; the provider's archive period does not delete those emails. You can use the contact form to request deletion or ask about your information. Please avoid sensitive personal information. See FormSubmit's privacy policy for its handling of data and third-party services, including spam prevention.

Recently viewed, collections and offline recipes

Recently viewed is optional and off until you enable it or accept that optional setting. It stores only the last eight distinct recipe filenames, in order, under adam-cooks-recent-v1. It does not send your reading history to Adam or analytics. Pause it in My cookbook or Privacy settings; pausing keeps older entries without reading or updating them. Clear history removes them.

Creating or editing a collection requests local saving under adam-cooks-collections-v1. This stores collection names and recipe filenames until you delete them or clear browser/app data. It is separate from optional history, planner, prompt and analytics choices. Export backup lets you download or copy a JSON backup; Import backup merges valid collections without deleting existing lists. Backups do not contain private cooking notes or offline downloads.

Pressing Save for offline requests an offline copy of the selected public recipe, its pictures and supporting files, plus the My cookbook interface. A service worker serves downloaded files when a connection is unavailable. Downloads and their saved dates are held in browser caches named adam-cooks-shell-…, adam-cooks-recipe-… and adam-cooks-download-index-v1. Browsing another recipe does not automatically download it. Use Update copy or Remove download in My cookbook; removing downloads does not remove your collections, favourites or cooking notes. AI requests and contact submissions are not cached by this feature.

If you choose Maybe later on the home-screen invitation, adam-cooks-install-later-v1 remembers that choice in this browser for 14 days. No preference is sent to Adam. A home-screen icon provides quick access and does not itself download recipes. These features are local to the browser or installed app and do not automatically sync between devices; different browsers or app installations may hold separate data. Clearing site/app data, removing an app or browser storage management can remove saved data. Keep a collections backup and check Available offline before relying on a download. Offline recipes can be older than the live site until you update their saved copies.

Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the website and images. GitHub and Cloudflare receive technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure their services. See GitHub's privacy statement. Browser-based plans and drafts are not uploaded to a user account or synced by Adam Cooks.

Visitor measurement

With your separate consent, Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-002QLTW2TV) helps us understand visits, popular pages and broad usage patterns. The Google tag is not loaded until analytics is accepted. Planner and prompt-saving consent from before this change does not enable analytics.

Google receives technical information about your browser and device and processes network information to provide Analytics. Our tracking configuration removes query strings and fragments from page addresses, limits referrers to their origin, and does not send cooking prompts, meal plans, names or contact details as custom analytics data. Google signals and advertising personalisation are disabled in our tag configuration; advertising consent remains denied.

You can switch analytics off using at any time. This stops our tag sending further Analytics data and removes its accessible first-party Analytics cookies. It does not recall data already sent. Cookie deletion can also be managed in your browser. Google controls server-side retention according to the property's settings; standard aggregated reports are treated separately. Read Google's privacy policy and Analytics data safeguards for more information. Google may process information outside the UK under its applicable safeguards and terms.

Advertising

AdSense site verification and an ads.txt authorisation file are installed, but advertising scripts and ad slots are not enabled. Analytics consent does not permit advertising. Before personalised Google advertising is enabled, we will configure an appropriate Google-certified consent platform and update this notice.

Your choices and questions

Optional browser saving and analytics use your consent, which you can withdraw in privacy settings. Requested AI processing is used to provide the service you ask for; technical security and usage limits protect the site and its budget. You can ask about your data, request access or deletion where applicable, and complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

For website feedback, recipe requests or privacy questions, contact Adam privately. Local-only plans and prompts are held in your browser; Adam cannot retrieve them from a central account.
