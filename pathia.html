Chicken Pathia

A hot, sweet and sour chicken curry with tomato, tamarind, mango chutney and lemon.

Estimated total: About 40 min totalBase gravy must already be prepared, diluted and hot before measuring. The time below includes preparing fresh chicken, but excludes making base gravy and any rice or naan. Cook larger quantities in batches.

Method

  1. 1

    Have the base gravy ready first

    Make the base gravy first using the base gravy recipe linked above. Dilute it until pourable, heat it, then measure the amount listed for this curry. Keep it hot while you prepare the chicken and sauce.
  2. 2

    Prepare the chicken

    Cut the raw chicken into bite-size pieces. Fry in its measured oil for 8–10 minutes, turning, until cooked through. Check the thickest pieces reach 75°C for 30 seconds. Keep hot and continue cooking this curry immediately.
  3. 3

    Prepare the tomato

    Mix the double-concentrated tomato purée with its measured water. Keep the diluted base gravy hot.
  4. 4

    Start the sauce

    Heat the neutral oil listed for the curry. Briefly fry the cassia bark and optional Asian bay leaf. Add the onion and soften it, then fry the ginger/garlic paste.
  5. 5

    Add spices and chicken

    Add the chilli powder, Kashmiri chilli powder, curry mix powder, smoked paprika, kasuri methi and salt, with the fresh coriander stalks and a splash of base gravy. Stir in the diluted tomato purée and fry briefly. Then add all the chicken pre-cooked earlier and coat it in the sauce.
  6. 6

    Reduce the gravy

    Add the remaining base gravy in roughly one-quarter, one-quarter and one-half portions, letting the sauce thicken between additions and stirring enough to avoid burning.
  7. 7

    Balance and serve

    With the last base gravy, add the mango chutney, brown sugar, lemon juice, tamarind sauce and optional Worcestershire sauce. Simmer until glossy and piping hot. Finish with the fresh coriander leaves, taste and adjust, then remove the cassia bark and any Asian bay leaf.

The tamarind measurement is for ready-to-use tamarind sauce, not concentrated paste.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool promptly and refrigerate covered within 2 hours. Use within 48 hours, or sooner if the base gravy has an earlier storage limit.

Freezing

Freeze portions for later reheating only when made with freshly cooked chicken and freshly made gravy in this cooking session. Otherwise freeze the gravy separately before finishing the curry. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours.

Reheating

Reheat once until steaming hot throughout. If chilled chicken or gravy was already reheated when making this curry, do not reheat the finished curry again.

Substitutions

If using tamarind concentrate, start with ½ tsp for 2 servings instead of the 2 tsp sauce and taste before adding more. Omit optional Worcestershire for a fish-free version; standard Worcestershire contains anchovies.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
