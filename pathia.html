Pathia Curry

Sweet, sour and spicy tomato-based curry.

Method

  1. 1

    Heat oil and fry the whole spices briefly to flavour the oil.

  2. 2

    Add onion and cook until soft and lightly browned.

  3. 3

    Add ginger/garlic paste and cook until the raw smell goes.

  4. 4

    Add the spices and fry briefly, using a little base gravy to stop burning.

  5. 5

    Add tomato paste and cook on high heat until the oil separates.

  6. 6

    Add your main ingredient and coat in the sauce.

  7. 7

    Add base gravy gradually, letting the sauce reduce between additions.

  8. 8

    Stir in mango chutney, sugar, tamarind and lemon juice.

  9. 9

    Simmer until thick, glossy, and balanced sweet/sour.

Tips & leftovers

Storage

Cool and refrigerate within 2 hours; use within 48 hours, or sooner if the main ingredient was cooked earlier. The recipe heats pre-cooked ingredients: do not plan another reheating of those ingredients.

Freezing

For batch cooking, freeze the sauce before adding the pre-cooked meat, cream or egg. Use within 2 months for best quality; add freshly prepared ingredients when finishing.

Reheating

Reheat sauce-only portions once until steaming hot, then finish with freshly cooked chicken breast or vegetables. If the finished curry already reheated its main ingredient, eat safely chilled leftovers cold rather than heating them again.

Substitutions

Use chicken breast or cooked vegetables as the main ingredient. Add lemon and tamarind gradually to balance the sweetness; substituting extra lemon for tamarind changes the flavour.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
