Aubergine Parmigiana

Roasted aubergine layered with tomato, basil, mozzarella and Parmesan. Serves 2.

Prep20 mins
CookAbout 55 mins

Method

  1. 1

    Roast the aubergines

    Heat the oven to 220°C / 200°C fan / gas 7. Cut the aubergines lengthways into slices about 8mm thick. Arrange on lined baking trays, brush with three-quarters of the oil and season. Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway, until soft and lightly golden.
  2. 2

    Make the tomato sauce

    Meanwhile, heat the remaining oil in a saucepan. Soften the onion over a medium heat for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, then add the tomatoes and oregano. Simmer uncovered for 15 minutes until thick. Taste and season.
  3. 3

    Prepare to layer

    Lower the oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / gas 6. Make sure the mozzarella is well drained. Spread a little sauce over the base of an approximately 20cm baking dish for 2 servings.
  4. 4

    Build the layers

    Add a layer of aubergine, then some tomato sauce, torn basil, mozzarella and grated hard cheese. Repeat, finishing with sauce and cheese on top. For more servings, use a larger dish or several dishes to keep similar layer depths.
  5. 5

    Bake

    Bake for 25–30 minutes until bubbling and golden. If the top colours too quickly, loosely cover with foil.
  6. 6

    Rest

    Let the parmigiana stand for 10 minutes before serving, so the layers hold together.
