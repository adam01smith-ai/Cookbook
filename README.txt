MY COOKBOOK

45 recipe pages and index.html, with AI-generated illustrative dish photos.

Extract the entire ZIP. Keep the images folder beside the HTML files. Open index.html to browse. When uploading to your website, upload all HTML files and the images folder, preserving its name.

Recipe pages retain ingredient checkboxes, the servings slider, printing and numbered methods. Photos are illustrations, not records of cooked results. The index includes photo thumbnails, category filters and search.
