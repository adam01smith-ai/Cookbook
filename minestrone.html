Minestrone

Classic Italian vegetable soup with beans, pasta and greens.

Method

  1. 1
    Heat olive oil in a large saucepan.
  2. 2
    Add onion, celery, carrot, courgette and pancetta.
  3. 3
    Cook gently for 10 minutes until softened.
  4. 4
    Stir in garlic and oregano and cook for 1 minute.
  5. 5
    Add beans, tomatoes, purée, stock and bay leaf.
  6. 6
    Season and simmer for 30 minutes.
  7. 7
    Add pasta and greens.
  8. 8
    Cook for a further 10 minutes.
  9. 9
    Serve with basil, parmesan and a drizzle of olive oil.

Add extra stock if the soup thickens too much while cooking.

Tastes even better the next day

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool in shallow containers, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions in sealed, labelled containers for up to 2 months for best quality. Leave expansion space. For better texture freeze the soup before adding pasta and greens, then add them fresh.

Reheating

Reheat once in a saucepan, stirring and adding a splash of water or stock, until steaming hot throughout. The pasta absorbs stock, so add extra liquid as needed.

Substitutions

Use seasonal vegetables and small pasta shapes. Omit pancetta and choose vegetarian stock and cheese for a vegetarian version; add extra beans for body.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
