Broccoli Quiche

Easy quiche using store-bought pastry with broccoli and cheese.

Method

  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. 2
    If using raw pastry, line a baking dish and blind bake for 10–15 minutes.
  3. 3
    Cook broccoli in boiling water for a few minutes until just tender, then drain well.
  4. 4
    Heat butter or oil in a pan and soften the onion.
  5. 5
    Whisk together eggs, milk, salt and pepper.
  6. 6
    Add broccoli, onion and cheese into the pastry case.
  7. 7
    Pour over the egg mixture.
  8. 8
    Bake for 35–45 minutes until set and golden.
  9. 9
    Cool slightly before slicing and serving.

Let it rest before cutting — helps it set properly like a classic quiche.

Tips & leftovers

Storage

Cool in shallow portions and refrigerate covered within 2 hours. Eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled individual portions, tightly wrapped, for up to 2 months for best quality. Defrost fully in the fridge. Wrap cooled slices individually; pastry can soften after thawing.

Reheating

Reheat once in an oven at 180°C / 160°C fan until steaming hot in the centre. Cover if the top browns before the middle is hot; portion size affects the time. Heat until the egg filling is hot in the centre; protect pastry edges if they brown first.

Substitutions

Use well-drained spinach or cooked leeks instead of broccoli. Gruyère can replace cheddar. Keep the egg-and-milk balance; a smaller tin may be needed when reducing servings.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
