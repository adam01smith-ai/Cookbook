Slow Cooker Smoky Hot Chilli Con Carne

A rich, spicy beef chilli with kidney beans, fresh red chillies, smoky chipotle and a little dark chocolate.

Time to allow: 6 hr 15 min–8 hr 15 min totalLow setting; high setting takes about 3¼–4¼ hours including prep.

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil in a frying pan over a medium-high heat. Add the mince and onion and fry for 6–8 minutes, breaking up the mince, until browned.

  2. 2

    Add the garlic, tomato purée, cumin, paprika, coriander, hot chilli powder, fresh chillies, chipotle paste, optional cayenne and oregano. Stir for 1 minute.

  3. 3

    Transfer to the slow cooker. Add the pepper, chopped tomatoes, kidney beans, stock cube, Worcestershire sauce and sugar. Stir together. Do not add water; the vegetables release liquid as they cook.

  4. 4

    Cover and cook on low for 6–8 hours or high for 3–4 hours.

  5. 5

    Stir in the dark chocolate during the final 15 minutes. If you want it thicker, cook on high with the lid off for the final 20–30 minutes, if your slow cooker’s instructions allow.

  6. 6

    Taste and season with salt and pepper. Fresh chillies vary in strength, so taste before adding any extra cayenne.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Divide into shallow containers to cool, refrigerate within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions with their sauce for up to 2 months for best quality. Label before freezing. Freeze before adding any yoghurt, cheese or other fresh toppings.

Reheating

Reheat once on the hob or in the microwave until steaming hot throughout; stir and turn meat pieces so the centre heats evenly. Do not use the slow cooker to reheat chilled leftovers. Add a splash of water if the thick sauce catches; serve with freshly prepared toppings.

Substitutions

Use black beans instead of kidney beans. For a milder chilli, reduce the fresh chillies, hot chilli powder and chipotle; extra chocolate does not remove heat.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
