Pea & Asparagus Soup

Smooth, fresh pea and asparagus soup finished with cream and chives.

8person
2712

Ingredient quantities update automatically

IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Heat olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Add onion and garlic and cook gently for 10 minutes until soft.

  3. 3

    Add stock and bring to the boil.

  4. 4

    Add peas. When boiling again, add asparagus.

  5. 5

    Simmer for 20 minutes until very soft.

  6. 6

    Blend until smooth, then strain through a sieve.

  7. 7

    Stir in half the cream and season to taste.

  8. 8

    Reheat if serving hot, or chill if serving cold.

  9. 9

    Mix chives into remaining cream and swirl into soup before serving.
