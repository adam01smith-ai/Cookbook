Pea & Asparagus Soup

Smooth, fresh pea and asparagus soup finished with cream and chives.

Estimated total: About 50 min totalFor serving hot; allow extra chilling time if serving cold.

Method

  1. 1

    Heat olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Add onion and garlic and cook gently for 10 minutes until soft.

  3. 3

    Add stock and bring to the boil.

  4. 4

    Add peas. When boiling again, add asparagus.

  5. 5

    Simmer for 20 minutes until very soft.

  6. 6

    Blend until smooth, then strain through a sieve.

  7. 7

    Stir in half the cream and season to taste.

  8. 8

    Reheat if serving hot, or chill if serving cold.

  9. 9

    Mix chives into remaining cream and swirl into soup before serving.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool in shallow containers, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours. Label the container with the recipe name and the date and time it was made; follow any shorter ingredient use-by or opened-pack instructions.

Freezing

Freezing not recommended once the cream has been added. For batch cooking, freeze the cooled soup base without cream for up to 2 months for best quality; defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat once until steaming hot and add fresh cream when serving.

Reheating

Reheat once in a saucepan, stirring and adding a splash of water or stock, until steaming hot throughout. Add fresh chives at the end.

Substitutions

Use vegetable stock instead of chicken stock. Swap asparagus for tender broccoli, cooking until soft before blending. Oat cream can replace double cream, with a different flavour.

Keeping leftovers safe

Keep chilled food at 0–5°C and follow the shortest ingredient use-by or opened-pack instruction. Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Reheat suitable cooked leftovers only once, until steaming hot throughout; ingredients already reheated in this recipe must not be reheated again. Serve chilled desserts cold. Freezing recommendations and any exceptions for a base prepared without cream or prawns are explained in this recipe’s Freezing note.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
