Slow Cooker Beef Bourguignon

Tender beef in a rich red wine gravy with bacon, mushrooms and shallots. The full recipe serves 6; quantities default to 6 servings.

Method

  1. 1

    Brown the beef

    Turn the slow cooker to Low. Heat 2 tbsp of the vegetable oil in a large frying pan. Season the beef and brown in batches for 3–4 minutes, turning to colour all sides. Lift each batch out with a slotted spoon and set aside on a plate.
  2. 2

    Build the sauce and slow cook

    Add the chopped onion, carrot and celery to the pan and fry for 5–10 minutes until softened. Add the bay leaves, thyme or rosemary, and flour; cook for another 2 minutes. Mix a splash of the red wine with the stock cube, sugar and tomato purée to form a paste. Stir the paste into the vegetables, then add the remaining wine and bring to a bubble. Transfer to the slow cooker and stir in the browned beef and any juices. Add only a little water if needed to just cover the meat. Cover and cook on Low for 6–8 hours until the beef is tender but still holds its shape.
  3. 3

    Add bacon, shallots and mushrooms

    About 35 minutes before serving, heat the remaining 1 tbsp vegetable oil in a frying pan. Fry the bacon lardons, shallots and mushrooms for 5–8 minutes until caramelised and starting to soften. Add to the slow cooker, turn to High and cook for 30 minutes. For a thicker gravy, use the cooker’s reduce function or cook uncovered until thickened. Remove the bay leaves and woody herb stalks before serving.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Divide into shallow containers to cool, refrigerate within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions with their sauce for up to 2 months for best quality. Label before freezing. Mushrooms and onions may soften but the sauce freezes well.

Reheating

Reheat once on the hob or in the microwave until steaming hot throughout; stir and turn meat pieces so the centre heats evenly. Do not use the slow cooker to reheat chilled leftovers. Turn larger pieces of beef and check they are hot all the way through.

Substitutions

Use braising or stewing beef, not quick-fry steak. Beef stock can replace wine, giving a different flavour. Chestnut mushrooms work instead of closed-cup mushrooms.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.
