Indonesian Chicken Poke Bowl

Jasmine rice with spiced air-fried chicken, sweet potato, crunchy cabbage and edamame, finished with sambal sweet chilli sauce.

Method

  1. 1
    Cut the sweet potato into 2 cm chunks. Place in the air fryer, drizzle with oil and season with salt, pepper and half the Indonesian spice mix. Shake to coat, then cook for about 20–25 minutes, shaking during cooking, until tender.
  2. 2
    Dice the chicken breasts. Place in a separate air fryer basket or cook in a separate batch. Drizzle with oil and season with salt, pepper and the remaining Indonesian spice mix. Shake to coat, then cook for about 16–18 minutes, turning during cooking, until cooked through. Air fryer temperatures were not specified; use your appliance’s instructions for chicken and sweet potato, and adjust the timings as needed.
  3. 3
    Prepare the edamame according to the packet instructions if cooking is required. In a medium bowl, mix the soy sauce, rice vinegar and a little olive oil. Add the red cabbage and edamame, stir to coat, then season with salt and pepper.
  4. 4
    In a small bowl, mix the sambal paste and sweet chilli sauce.
  5. 5
    Cook the jasmine rice according to the packet instructions, then divide between two bowls, or your chosen number of servings.
  6. 6
    Top the rice with the chicken, sweet potato and dressed cabbage and edamame. Drizzle over the sambal sweet chilli sauce and serve.

Tips & leftovers

Storage

Cool rice as quickly as possible, ideally within 1 hour, and refrigerate in shallow portions. Eat the rice, and any leftovers mixed with it, within 24 hours. Keep other cooked components separate and use within 48 hours. Store cabbage, edamame and sauce separately for a fresher texture.

Freezing

Freeze suitable cooled components separately for up to 2 months for best quality. If freezing freshly cooked rice, cool it quickly and freeze within 1 hour. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Do not freeze the dressed cabbage. Microwave rice that has already been heated should not be saved for reheating again.

Reheating

Reheat rice only once until steaming hot throughout and eat immediately. Heat other cooked components thoroughly, keeping cold toppings separate. Make fresh rice if the microwave rice has already been heated once. Reheat the chicken and sweet potato separately before adding the chilled toppings.

Substitutions

Swap sweet potato for squash, allowing for its cooking time. Use peas instead of edamame, cooking them as directed. Reduce the sambal for less heat and keep chicken breast as the meat.

Keeping leftovers safe

Rice needs faster cooling: cool quickly, ideally within 1 hour, and refrigerate; use within 24 hours. If freezing freshly cooked rice, cool it quickly and freeze within 1 hour. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once until steaming hot throughout and eat immediately. Do not heat rice again if it was already reheated, including a heated microwave pouch. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers · cooked rice.
