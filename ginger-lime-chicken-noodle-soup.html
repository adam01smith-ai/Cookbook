Ginger and Lime Chicken Noodle Soup

Chicken, udon noodles and spinach in a coconut broth with ginger, garlic and lime.

Method

  1. 1
    Slice the bell pepper into thin strips.
  2. 2
    Heat a drizzle of oil in a pan over a high heat. Once hot, add the pepper and stir-fry until charred.
  3. 3
    Add the tomato purée, ginger and garlic paste, and Thai spice mix. Fry for 30 seconds.
  4. 4
    Stir in the coconut milk, soy sauce, honey and water.
  5. 5
    Bring to the boil, then lower the heat to a simmer.
  6. 6
    Add the diced chicken and simmer for about 10–12 minutes, or until fully cooked through. The exact time will depend on the size of the pieces.
  7. 7
    Cut the lime into wedges.
  8. 8
    Once the chicken is cooked, add the udon noodles and the spinach, a handful at a time. Cook until the spinach has wilted and the noodles are hot and cooked according to their packet instructions.
  9. 9
    Squeeze in the juice from half the lime, stir the broth, then season with salt and pepper. Serve with the remaining lime wedges.

Tips & leftovers

Storage

Cool in shallow containers, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours. Noodles absorb broth, so keep them separate when preparing ahead.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions in sealed, labelled containers for up to 2 months for best quality. Leave expansion space. Freeze the chicken broth without noodles and spinach for a better texture; add those fresh.

Reheating

Reheat once in a saucepan, stirring and adding a splash of water or stock, until steaming hot throughout. Make sure chicken pieces are hot in the centre, then add freshly cooked noodles and spinach.

Substitutions

Use chicken breast as written. Pak choi can replace spinach; add its stems before the leaves. Rice noodles work instead of udon, but cook them according to their own packet.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
