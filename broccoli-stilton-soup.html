Broccoli & Stilton Soup

Creamy soup with broccoli and rich blue cheese flavour.

Method

  1. 1

    Heat oil in a pan and cook the onion until soft.

  2. 2

    Add celery, leek, potato and butter. Stir until melted, cover and cook gently for 5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Pour in the stock and add the broccoli stalk pieces. Cook for 10–15 minutes until soft.

  4. 4

    Add the rest of the broccoli and cook for another 5 minutes.

  5. 5

    Blend until smooth.

  6. 6

    Stir in the stilton, leaving a few small chunks if you like.

  7. 7

    Season with black pepper and serve.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool in shallow containers, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions in sealed, labelled containers for up to 2 months for best quality. Leave expansion space. The cheese may separate slightly; blend briefly after reheating if needed.

Reheating

Reheat once in a saucepan, stirring and adding a splash of water or stock, until steaming hot throughout. Warm gently and stir well rather than boiling hard.

Substitutions

Use another blue cheese instead of Stilton, or mature cheddar for a milder soup. Vegetable stock makes it meat-free; choose vegetarian-labelled cheese if required.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
