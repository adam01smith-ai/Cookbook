Roasted Red Pepper Soup

Deep, smoky soup made with roasted peppers and chopped tomatoes.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. 2
    Place peppers, onion (cut side down) and garlic on a baking tray. Drizzle with olive oil.
  3. 3
    Roast for 30 minutes until soft and slightly charred.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, melt butter in a large pan and gently cook celery for 4–5 minutes.
  5. 5
    Mix the stock with tomato purée, sundried tomato paste and chilli flakes, then add to the pan along with the chopped tomatoes.
  6. 6
    Peel the roasted onion and garlic, roughly chop, and add them to the pan along with the roasted peppers.
  7. 7
    Blend until smooth using a hand blender.
  8. 8
    Gently heat without boiling, then season to taste.
  9. 9
    Serve warm, ideally with crusty bread.

Roasting the vegetables gives this soup its deep flavour.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool in shallow containers, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions in sealed, labelled containers for up to 2 months for best quality. Leave expansion space. Freeze without any fresh garnish.

Reheating

Reheat once in a saucepan, stirring and adding a splash of water or stock, until steaming hot throughout. Add water or stock if the tomato-rich soup becomes too thick.

Substitutions

Drained jarred roasted peppers can replace freshly roasted peppers; reduce any added salt. Use olive oil instead of butter for a dairy-free version, checking the stock label.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
