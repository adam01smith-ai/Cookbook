Jack Daniel’s Style BBQ Sauce

Sweet, smoky, sticky glaze — perfect for chicken, ribs, burgers or loaded potatoes.

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1
    Add water, pineapple juice, teriyaki sauce, soy sauce and brown sugar to a saucepan.
  2. 2
    Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally.
  3. 3
    Reduce heat and let it gently simmer.
  4. 4
    Add minced garlic and whisk to combine.
  5. 5
    Add olive oil, lemon juice, onion, whiskey, crushed pineapple and cayenne pepper.
  6. 6
    Stir everything together.
  7. 7
    Simmer for 40–50 minutes until reduced by about half and thick and syrupy.

Don’t let it boil over. Sauce thickens further as it cools.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool promptly and refrigerate in a clean covered container within 2 hours. Use within 48 hours; this homemade sauce is not shelf-stable.

Freezing

Freeze cooled sauce in small labelled portions for up to 2 months for best quality. Leave room for expansion. The pineapple pieces will soften but remain suitable for a glaze.

Reheating

Reheat only the amount needed, once, in a saucepan, stirring until steaming hot throughout. Add a little water if too thick. Stir often because the sugary glaze catches easily.

Substitutions

Replace whiskey with extra pineapple juice for an alcohol-free variation, and check the other sauces. Simmer to the desired consistency; do not assume brief cooking removes all alcohol.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
