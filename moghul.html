Chicken Moghul

A mild, creamy chicken curry with almonds, red pepper and fragrant whole spices.

Method

  1. 1

    Have the base gravy ready first

    Make the base gravy first using the base gravy recipe linked above. Dilute it until pourable, heat it, then measure the amount listed for this curry. Keep it hot while you prepare the chicken and sauce.
  2. 2

    Prepare the chicken

    Cut the raw chicken into bite-size pieces. Fry in its measured oil for 8–10 minutes, turning, until cooked through. Check the thickest pieces reach 75°C for 30 seconds. Keep hot and continue cooking this curry immediately.
  3. 3

    Prepare optional egg

    If using the egg, hard-boil it in water for 9–10 minutes, cool under running water and peel. This can happen while the chicken cooks.
  4. 4

    Start the sauce

    Heat the neutral oil listed for the curry. Briefly fry the cassia bark, cloves and green cardamom pods. Add the red pepper and soften it, then stir in the fresh garlic and fresh ginger.
  5. 5

    Add almonds and spices

    Stir in the kasuri methi, curry mix powder, turmeric, chilli powder, salt, ground almonds and white sugar with one-quarter of the base gravy. Stir to prevent the ground almonds catching.
  6. 6

    Add the pre-cooked chicken to the sauce

    Once the ground almonds and named seasonings have cooked into the sauce, add all the pre-cooked chicken prepared earlier and stir to coat it. Add the remaining base gravy, lemon juice and optional Worcestershire sauce. Reduce until the sauce coats the chicken and is piping hot throughout.
  7. 7

    Finish gently

    Reserve a teaspoon of the measured single cream per serving for garnish. Lower the heat, stir in the rest of the single cream and optional sultanas, then gently heat through. Remove the cassia bark, cloves and green cardamom pods. Add the optional butter ghee. Garnish with the reserved single cream, garam masala, fresh coriander leaves and the optional egg, divided between servings.

The chicken is weighed raw. The cream includes the garnish; no unmeasured extra cream is needed. Optional egg is listed as a whole egg so it can be bought.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool promptly and refrigerate covered within 2 hours. Use within 48 hours, or sooner if the base gravy has an earlier storage limit.

Freezing

Freeze portions for later reheating only when made with freshly cooked chicken and freshly made gravy in this cooking session. Otherwise freeze the gravy separately before finishing the curry. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours.

Reheating

Reheat once until steaming hot throughout. If chilled chicken or gravy was already reheated when making this curry, do not reheat the finished curry again.

Substitutions

Contains almonds and dairy. Omit optional Worcestershire for a fish-free version; standard Worcestershire contains anchovies. Optional sultanas, ghee and egg stay out of the shopping list unless selected.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
