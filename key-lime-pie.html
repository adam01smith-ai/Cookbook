Key Lime Pie

A crisp oatmeal biscuit base with tangy lime filling and softly whipped cream. The full recipe makes one 22cm pie, cut into 8 portions. Opens at 2 servings.

Prep30 mins
Bake25 mins
ChillAt least 6 hrs
2people

Ingredients

For part of an egg yolk, beat a yolk and weigh the required fraction. Use the tin guide in the method; smaller pies may bake faster.

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oven

    Heat the oven to 160°C / 140°C fan / gas 3.
  2. 2

    Crush the biscuits

    Whizz 75 g oatmeal biscuits into crumbs in a food processor, or put in a strong plastic bag and crush with a rolling pin.
  3. 3

    Make and bake the base

    Mix the crumbs with 27.5 g melted butter. Press into the base and up the sides of a loose-based tart tin, approximately 11 cm in diameter for the selected servings (22 cm for 8 servings). Bake for 10 minutes, checking smaller bases earlier, then remove and cool.
  4. 4

    Whisk the yolks

    Put 0.75 medium egg yolks in a large bowl and whisk for 1 minute with electric beaters.
  5. 5

    Make the filling

    Add 99.25 g condensed milk and whisk for 3 minutes. Add the finely grated zest and juice of 1 limes and whisk for another 3 minutes.
  6. 6

    Bake and chill

    Pour the filling into the cooled base and return to the oven for about 15 minutes, until just set; check smaller pies earlier. Cool, then chill for at least 6 hours or overnight. When ready to serve, carefully remove the pie from the tin and place on a serving plate.
  7. 7

    Whip the cream

    Softly whip together 75 ml double cream and 0.25 tbsp icing sugar.
  8. 8

    Decorate

    Dollop or pipe the cream over the pie and finish with extra lime zest.

Tips & leftovers

Storage

Keep covered in the fridge at 0–5°C and eat within 48 hours of making. Refrigerate promptly after cooling and return leftovers to the fridge straight after serving; follow any shorter dairy use-by date.

Freezing

Freeze the cooled pie without whipped cream, well wrapped, for up to 1 month for best texture. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Add fresh whipped cream and zest before serving; the base may be softer after thawing.

Reheating

Serve chilled. Do not reheat the finished dessert.

Substitutions

Use digestive biscuits instead of oatmeal biscuits if preferred. Ordinary limes work; lemon gives a different pie. Use full-fat condensed milk as written, not evaporated milk.

Keeping leftovers safe

Keep chilled food at 0–5°C and follow the shortest ingredient use-by or opened-pack instruction. For cooked desserts, cool promptly and refrigerate within 2 hours; do not leave them out overnight. Freeze suitable portions promptly, keep the freezer around −18°C, defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Do not refreeze a thawed serving.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
