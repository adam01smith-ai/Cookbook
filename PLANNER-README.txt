MEAL PLANNER

Open meal-planner.html, or use Meal planner at the top of the recipe index.

Choose 0-7 cookbook dinners, 1-12 people and a repeat gap of 1, 2 or 4 weeks. Day types are cookbook recipe, eating out/takeaway, leftovers, own meal and unplanned. Keep preserves a meal; Swap changes one. Confirm saves the menu to browser history. Confirmed meals block repeats until marked skipped.

History is per device/browser, not an online account. Other visitors have independent plans. Export/import JSON backups from History to move data between browsers. Clearing site data removes local history. No automatic cross-device or household sync is provided.

35 recipes are eligible dinner choices (34 with No fish enabled). Base gravy, vinaigrette, BBQ sauce, orange chicken sauce, acai bowl and desserts are excluded. Ragu is paired with pasta. Main dishes can have a chosen side; soup can be paired with bread.

Side defaults per person: 75g dry rice or pasta, 250g potatoes, 2 slices bread. These are planner defaults; edit them on the meal. Linked leftovers add extra portions to an earlier cookbook cooking day.

The shopping list scales source quantities and combines matching ingredients in compatible units. Optional ingredients are excluded until selected. Unspecified recipe quantities are labelled Check quantity; no amount is invented. Different units or forms may remain separate. Whole items and counted packs are rounded up after combining; the contents of an unspecified pack are not guessed. Loose herb measures become bunch/pack shopping prompts, and other vague measures are flagged without invented weights. Recipe cooking quantities remain unchanged. Own-meal ingredients are entered one per line, for the number of people shown beside the input, and then scale with people.

INSTALL UPDATE: upload index.html, meal-planner.html, recipe-data.js, planner-core.js and planner-app.js to the same folder as existing recipe HTML files. Keep your existing images folder. No server setup or installation is needed.
`nPLAIN TEXT: in Shopping list, choose Plain text, then Copy shopping list. The export has one item per line, excludes checked items, and includes extra shopping. Select all is available if automatic clipboard access is blocked.
