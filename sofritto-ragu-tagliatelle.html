Sofritto Ragù with Tagliatelle

Silky tagliatelle tossed with a rich, slow-cooked beef, pork and pancetta ragù, finished with Parmesan.

Method

  1. 1
    Make the sofritto ragù first, using the linked recipe scaled to your chosen servings, or use ragù you have already prepared. Remove the bay leaves and woody herb stalks. Keep the ragù gently simmering while you cook the pasta.
  2. 2
    Bring a large saucepan of salted water to the boil. Cook the tagliatelle for 1 minute less than its packet instructions.
  3. 3
    Reserve a mug of pasta water before draining.
  4. 4
    Add the tagliatelle directly to the ragù with 2 tablespoons of pasta water for two servings; increase that starting amount for larger portions.
  5. 5
    Toss over medium heat for 1–2 minutes, adding more pasta water a tablespoon at a time until the sauce coats the ribbons.
  6. 6
    Take off the heat and stir through half the Parmesan. Taste and season.
  7. 7
    Divide between bowls and finish with the remaining Parmesan.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool promptly, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions for up to 2 months for best quality. Pasta softens after freezing; freezing the sauce separately gives a better result. Freeze the ragù separately and cook fresh pasta when serving for the best result.

Reheating

Reheat once in a pan with a splash of water, stirring until steaming hot throughout. Add fresh herbs or cheese after heating.

Substitutions

Use pappardelle or another broad pasta instead of tagliatelle. Stock can replace wine, and all-beef mince can replace the mixed mince.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
