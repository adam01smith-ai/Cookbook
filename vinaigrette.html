Classic formula: 3 parts oil : 1 part acid

Shake again before using if it separates.

Balsamic: use balsamic vinegar, reduce honey

Lemon: swap vinegar for lemon juice

Garlic: add 1 minced clove

Herb: parsley, basil or oregano

Tips & leftovers

Storage

Keep in a clean sealed jar in the fridge and use within 48 hours. Olive oil can turn cloudy or firm when chilled; shake before using. This dressing is not a shelf-stable preserve.

Freezing

Best mixed fresh rather than frozen; freezing can separate the dressing and dull its flavour.

Reheating

No reheating needed. Let only the portion you need loosen briefly at room temperature, then shake or whisk and use straight away.

Substitutions

Use lemon juice or a wine vinegar as written. Maple syrup can replace honey. Wholegrain mustard gives a grainier dressing; adjust the acidity and sweetness to taste.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
