Nacho Cheese Sauce

A smooth, creamy cheese sauce with a little cayenne heat. Makes 6 servings.

Estimated total: About 15 min total

2servings

IngredientsMethodTips & leftovers

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1
    Melt ⅔ tbsp salted butter in a saucepan over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Whisk in ⅔ tbsp all-purpose flour and 0.04 tsp cayenne pepper and cook for 1–2 minutes.
  3. 3
    Slowly whisk in 80 ml milk and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Reduce the heat to low and add 75 ⅔ g shredded sharp cheddar cheese or cubed processed American cheese.
  5. 5
    Stir until the cheese is melted and smooth.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool quickly and refrigerate covered within 2 hours. Use within 48 hours and follow any shorter milk storage instructions.

Freezing

Best made fresh. Freezing can make the cheese sauce grainy; if preparing ahead, refrigerate rather than freezing.

Reheating

Reheat once over a low heat, whisking in a splash of milk until smooth and steaming hot throughout. Do not boil vigorously or the cheese may split.

Substitutions

Use mature cheddar for a stronger flavour or processed American cheese for a smoother sauce. Reduce cayenne for less heat. Ordinary unsweetened milk works; plant milks will change the texture.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
