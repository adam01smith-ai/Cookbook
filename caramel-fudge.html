Caramel Fudge

Smooth, creamy fudge with a caramel swirl and a touch of sea salt.

Method

  1. 1
    Line a 20cm square tin with baking parchment.
  2. 2
    Add sugar, cream, butter and glucose syrup to a pan.
  3. 3
    Heat gently until sugar dissolves and butter melts.
  4. 4
    Increase heat and bring to a boil.
  5. 5
    Cook until mixture reaches 116°C (soft ball stage).
  6. 6
    Remove from heat and leave for 5 minutes.
  7. 7
    When temperature drops slightly, stir in vanilla and a pinch of salt.
  8. 8
    Beat vigorously until mixture thickens and loses its shine.
  9. 9
    Pour into tin and smooth the surface.
  10. 10
    Dot caramel on top and swirl through.
  11. 11
    Sprinkle sea salt over.
  12. 12
    Leave to cool until no longer hot, then cover and refrigerate within 2 hours. Chill overnight until set before cutting.
  13. 13
    Cut into squares.

Beating the mixture properly gives the fudge its smooth texture.

Use a thermometer for accuracy

Refrigeration makes the set fudge firmer; soften only the portion you want to eat.

Once set, refrigerate covered and eat within 48 hours; follow any shorter caramel storage instructions.

Tips & leftovers

Storage

Once set, keep covered in the fridge and eat within 48 hours, following any shorter storage instructions on the added caramel. The fridge makes the fudge firmer; take out only the portion you are about to eat.

Freezing

Wrap set squares well and freeze in a sealed container for up to 1 month for best texture. Defrost in the fridge, still wrapped, and use within 24 hours. Condensation may make the surface sticky.

Reheating

No reheating needed. Let a small serving soften briefly before eating. Re-melting the finished fudge can spoil its set and texture.

Substitutions

Use golden syrup instead of glucose syrup if necessary, expecting a slightly different flavour and set. Vanilla extract can replace paste in a smaller amount to taste. Follow the sugar temperature in the method.

Keeping leftovers safe

Keep chilled food at 0–5°C and follow the shortest ingredient use-by or opened-pack instruction. For cooked desserts, cool promptly and refrigerate within 2 hours; do not leave them out overnight. Freeze suitable portions promptly, keep the freezer around −18°C, defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Do not refreeze a thawed serving.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
