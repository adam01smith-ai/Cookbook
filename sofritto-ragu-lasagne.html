Sofritto Ragù Lasagne

Method

  1. 1
    Make the sofritto ragù first, using the linked recipe scaled to your chosen servings, or use ragù you have already prepared. Finish simmering uncovered until thick, with no watery liquid pooling around the meat. Remove the bay leaves and woody herb stalks.
  2. 2
    Heat the oven to 180°C fan / 200°C conventional. For two servings, use a baking dish approximately 20 × 15 cm. Use a larger dish when increasing the servings.
  3. 3
    Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Stir in the flour and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. 4
    Gradually whisk in the milk. Simmer gently for 5–7 minutes, stirring, until smooth and thick enough to coat a spoon.
  5. 5
    Stir in half the Parmesan, the nutmeg and a little salt and pepper.
  6. 6
    Spread a thin layer of ragù over the bottom of the dish. Add lasagne sheets, another layer of ragù and some white sauce. Repeat, finishing with pasta covered completely by white sauce.
  7. 7
    Scatter over the remaining Parmesan. Cover loosely with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  8. 8
    Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes, until golden, bubbling and the pasta is tender when pierced. Follow the sheets’ packet instructions if they require a longer cooking time.
  9. 9
    Rest for 10–15 minutes before cutting.

Tips & leftovers

Storage

Cool in shallow portions and refrigerate covered within 2 hours. Eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled individual portions, tightly wrapped, for up to 2 months for best quality. Defrost fully in the fridge. Freeze in individual portions with enough sauce to keep the pasta moist.

Reheating

Reheat once in an oven at 180°C / 160°C fan until steaming hot in the centre. Cover if the top browns before the middle is hot; portion size affects the time.

Substitutions

Use stock instead of red wine. All-beef mince can replace the beef-and-pork mixture. Choose lasagne sheets that suit the method and follow any packet pre-cooking instructions.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
