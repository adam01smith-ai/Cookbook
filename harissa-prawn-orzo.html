Harissa Prawn Orzo

Creamy, spicy harissa orzo with prawns, green beans and parmesan.

Method

  1. 1
    Finely slice the onion and grate the garlic.
  2. 2
    Zest half the lemon and set aside. Cut the lemon into wedges.
  3. 3
    Trim the green beans and cut into thirds.
  4. 4
    Cook the onion in a little oil until soft and lightly golden. Season well.
  5. 5
    Add the green beans and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  6. 6
    Add half the garlic and the orzo and stir for 1 minute.
  7. 7
    Pour in the vegetable stock and simmer gently.
  8. 8
    Cook until the orzo is al dente and most of the liquid has been absorbed.
  9. 9
    Add the prawns and cook until pink and cooked through.
  10. 10
    Stir in the harissa paste to taste.
  11. 11
    Add the parmesan and lemon zest.
  12. 12
    Finish with lemon juice to taste and serve.

Add extra stock if the orzo absorbs the liquid too quickly.

Add the harissa gradually until you reach your preferred heat level

Reserve a splash of stock for a looser, creamier texture

Finish with extra parmesan and lemon zest for serving

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool promptly, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours. If the prawns were already cooked before being added, eat the dish freshly made rather than planning another reheating. Label the container with the recipe name and the date and time it was made; follow any shorter ingredient use-by or opened-pack instructions.

Freezing

Freezing not recommended for this finished prawn dish. Make only the portions you need and follow the chilled leftover instructions.

Reheating

If the prawns were raw when added and cooked in this recipe, reheat a chilled portion only once, adding a splash of water and stirring until the orzo and the centres of the prawns are steaming hot throughout. If ready-cooked prawns were heated in the recipe, do not reheat the leftovers again; eat safely chilled leftovers cold within the storage limit. If unsure of the prawns' previous heating history, do not reheat.

Substitutions

Use small broccoli florets instead of green beans. Harissa varies in heat, so start with less. If using ready-cooked prawns, add only at the end to heat through and do not reheat them again.

Keeping leftovers safe

Keep chilled food at 0–5°C and follow the shortest ingredient use-by or opened-pack instruction. Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Reheat suitable cooked leftovers only once, until steaming hot throughout; ingredients already reheated in this recipe must not be reheated again. Serve chilled desserts cold. Freezing recommendations and any exceptions for a base prepared without cream or prawns are explained in this recipe’s Freezing note.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
