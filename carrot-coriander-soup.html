Carrot & Coriander Soup

Smooth, warming soup with sweet carrots and fresh coriander.

Method

  1. 1
    Heat oil in a large pan and cook the onion for about 5 minutes until softened.
  2. 2
    Add ground coriander and potato, then cook for 1 minute.
  3. 3
    Add carrots and stock. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat.
  4. 4
    Cover and simmer for about 20 minutes until the carrots are tender.
  5. 5
    Blend with the fresh coriander until smooth (in batches if needed).
  6. 6
    Return to the pan, season to taste and reheat gently before serving.

Blend in batches carefully — hot soup expands in a blender.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool in shallow containers, refrigerate covered within 2 hours and eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled portions in sealed, labelled containers for up to 2 months for best quality. Leave expansion space.

Reheating

Reheat once in a saucepan, stirring and adding a splash of water or stock, until steaming hot throughout.

Substitutions

Use vegetable stock for a vegetarian soup. Replace potato with a little extra carrot for a lighter result; blend well. Add fresh coriander after reheating for a brighter flavour.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
