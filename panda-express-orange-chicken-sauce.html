🍊 Panda Express Orange Chicken Sauce

🍊 Orange🍯 Sweet & sticky🌶️ Lightly spicy

A sweet, tangy orange sauce to coat fried chicken. Move the slider to adjust the ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 0.25 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp garlic, minced
  • 0.5 tsp ginger, minced
  • 0.25 cup sugar (50 g)
  • 0.25 cup brown sugar (55 g)
  • 0.25 cup orange juice (60 ml)
  • 0.25 cup white distilled vinegar (60 ml)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 2 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Method

  1. Heat a pan over a medium heat and add the oil.
  2. Once the oil begins to shimmer, add the chilli flakes, ginger and garlic. Cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
  3. Add the sugar and brown sugar, and stir to combine.
  4. Add the orange juice and allow the sugars to begin to dissolve, stirring occasionally.
  5. Add the vinegar and soy sauce, and stir to combine.
  6. Whisk the cornflour and water together in a separate bowl until smooth. Add to the pan and stir.
  7. Continue to cook the sauce until it reaches a maple syrup consistency.
  8. Add the fried chicken and stir until completely coated in the sauce.
  9. Top with sesame oil.

This recipe is for the sauce. Prepare your fried chicken separately.
