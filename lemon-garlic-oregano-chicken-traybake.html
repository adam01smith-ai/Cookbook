Lemon, Garlic & Oregano Chicken Traybake

Boneless chicken breast with golden potatoes, roasted red onion and a lemony garlic sauce—all cooked in one tray.

Time to allow: 50–55 min total

Prep10 mins

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 220°C / 200°C fan.

  2. 2

    Toss the potatoes with half the olive oil, the smoked paprika, salt and pepper in a large roasting tray. Spread into a single layer and roast for 15 minutes.

  3. 3

    Mix the remaining olive oil with the garlic, oregano, honey, finely grated lemon zest and juice of half the lemon.

  4. 4

    Add the chicken and onion to the tray. Spoon the lemon mixture over them and turn to coat. Keep the potatoes spread out and place the chicken flat between them.

  5. 5

    Roast for about 20–25 minutes, turning the potatoes halfway. Start checking the chicken earlier if the breasts are small: the thickest part must reach 75°C for at least 30 seconds. Continue cooking if needed; the potatoes should be golden and tender. If the potatoes need longer, remove the chicken and keep it warm while they finish.

  6. 6

    Squeeze over the remaining lemon to taste and spoon the tray juices over the chicken.

Tips & leftovers

Storage & leftovers

Cool in shallow portions and refrigerate covered within 2 hours. Eat within 48 hours.

Freezing

Freeze cooled individual portions, tightly wrapped, for up to 2 months for best quality. Defrost fully in the fridge. Potatoes lose some crispness; freeze with the chicken juices to reduce dryness.

Reheating

Reheat once in an oven at 180°C / 160°C fan until steaming hot in the centre. Cover if the top browns before the middle is hot; portion size affects the time. Add a splash of water and cover the chicken while heating; uncover potatoes at the end if desired.

Substitutions

Use chicken breast as written. Swap red onion for brown onion. Small potato chunks need a head start so the breast does not overcook.

Keeping leftovers safe

Cool cooked leftovers in small portions and refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the fridge at 0–5°C and the freezer around −18°C. Freeze suitable leftovers promptly. Defrost fully in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot all the way through. Follow any shorter use-by or opened-pack instructions; using older cooked ingredients does not restart their storage time.

Food-safety guidance: Food Standards Agency: chilling and freezing · reheating leftovers.
